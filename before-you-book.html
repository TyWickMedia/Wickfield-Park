Before You Book

One important thing, then straight to the calendar

Thirty seconds to read, and it saves the whole park a very bad week.

No chemically treated waste down our sewer

Every site here has a full sewer hookup, and all of it runs to an aerobic septic system. Live bacteria do the treating, and they're what keeps the system working for all eleven sites.

Formaldehyde-based holding tank deodorizers, bleach and other harsh chemicals kill those bacteria. When that happens the system stops treating, and fixing it is expensive, slow and unpleasant for every guest in the park — not just the one who dumped.

✓ Please use

Enzyme- or bacteria-based tank treatments. Look for "enzyme," "bio-active" or "septic safe" on the label.

✕ Please don't

Anything listing formaldehyde, plus bleach, harsh cleaners, solvents, paint, or grease.

Not sure what's already in your tank? Call us before you dump. We would far rather answer a question than rebuild a septic system.
